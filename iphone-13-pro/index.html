iPhone 13 Pro questions

How much is the iPhone 13 Pro at Techbrothers.za?

Current prices: 128GB certified pre-owned R7,899; 128GB brand new R8,599. Prices are in South African rand and valid until 31 December 2026.

Should I buy the iPhone 13 Pro pre-owned or brand new?

A certified pre-owned iPhone 13 Pro starts at R7,899, R700 less than brand new at R8,599. Brand new phones are sealed and come with a longer 6-month warranty; pre-owned phones come with a 30-day warranty.

What warranty comes with the iPhone 13 Pro?

Certified pre-owned phones come with a 30-day limited warranty. Brand new sealed phones come with a 6-month limited warranty. The warranty covers hardware faults such as charging, speaker and microphone faults that were not caused by damage. See the full Warranty & Returns policy for details.

Can I return or exchange the phone?

Returns or exchanges are accepted within 14 days of purchase for a verified technical fault that was not caused by the customer. A Techbrothers-approved technician inspects the phone first.

Do you deliver across South Africa?

Yes. Techbrothers delivers nationwide, to your door anywhere in South Africa.

How do I order and pay?

Choose your storage, condition and colour, add your name and number, then either pay by EFT straight away (the bank details and your order reference show on screen) or place the order and a Techbrothers representative contacts you to confirm stock and colour and arrange payment and delivery.
